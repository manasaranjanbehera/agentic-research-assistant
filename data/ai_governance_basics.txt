Production AI agent deployments in regulated industries typically require:
an audit trail of every tool call and model response; PII detection and
redaction before data leaves a trust boundary; policy checks that block or
flag disallowed actions before they execute (not just after); and cost and
usage tracking per agent, per user, or per business unit. These controls are
usually implemented as a wrapper layer around the model client rather than
inside the model itself, so they apply consistently regardless of which
foundation model is swapped in behind the agent.
